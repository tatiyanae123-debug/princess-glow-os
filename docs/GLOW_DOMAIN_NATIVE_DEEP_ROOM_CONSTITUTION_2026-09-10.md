# Glow OS — Domain-Native Deep Room Constitution

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE / PERMANENT  
**Date locked:** 2026-09-10  
**Scope:** Every current and future page, subpage, sub-subpage, object view, routine player, detail surface, archive, studio, planner, and spatial experience in Glow OS.

This constitution extends the Living Intelligence + Digital Twin Constitution, Glow Current Global Navigation Lock, Universal Shell Lock, and Global Intelligence Inheritance Lock.

## 1. Canonical deep-room flow

Every deep surface inherits the same conceptual architecture:

**Life Model → Glow Graph → Glow Intelligence Kernel → Room Experience → Action → Learning → History**

A deep page is never an isolated dataset. It is a contextual projection of one or more canonical Glow Objects through a domain-specific lens.

Example: `Life → Beauty → Skincare → Retinoids → Tretinoin` means the canonical Tretinoin Glow Object viewed through the Skincare lens in current life context. It does not create a page-local copy of product truth.

## 2. Generic deep-page composition is prohibited

Glow OS must not converge on the conventional architecture:

`World → Category → Subcategory → Tabs → Cards → Detail → Back`.

Shared primitives are required. Shared page composition is not.

Rooms may share typography, material tokens, motion physics, Glow Current, object controls, Ask Glow/Shakti, receipts, provenance, permissions, spacing and accessibility infrastructure. They must not all render as the same title + tabs + card grid + details template.

Domain-specific composition is mandatory.

## 3. Full-scroll art direction

A room is not considered Glow because its hero is beautiful. Art direction, hierarchy, material logic and spatial composition must remain intentional throughout the full scroll depth.

Do not allow the lower page to collapse into generic white rectangles, gray borders, pill tabs, uniform cards, database tables or ordinary accordions unless a specific functional task genuinely requires that representation.

## 4. Universal design DNA, local material identity

The shared visual family is Living Pearl Modernism evolving into an editorial digital-magazine language: mother-of-pearl, ivory stone, linen, water, clear/frosted glass, champagne metal, soft daylight, editorial typography, meaningful personal imagery, luxury flat lays, archival material, refined scrapbook moments, generous negative space and quiet refraction/motion.

Operational rooms remain calm and legible. Color remains an accent rather than a full-screen wash.

Each domain must be recognizable without its title:

- Today: temporal, atmospheric, present-tense
- Plan: future-state spatial reasoning
- Calendar: architectural time wall
- Routines: living multidimensional ritual system
- Skincare: translucent, wet, luminous, clinical-luxury treatment cabinet
- Hair: tactile, flowing, photographic, cyclical
- Makeup: editorial, pigment-driven, face/placement-oriented
- Beauty maintenance: recurrence, due-soon rhythm, body maintenance dossier
- Nails: miniature beauty dossier
- Brows + Lashes: face-specific maintenance map
- Fitness: architectural, bodily, kinetic; active player prioritizes utility
- Food: ingredient-rich editorial
- Closet: fashion-magazine atelier and composition workspace
- Money: calm, private, precise, comprehension-first
- Home: spatial, material and place-oriented
- Saint: warm, relational, personal, never cartoonish
- Brain: archival, cerebral, provenance-rich
- Create: spatial studio table with free composition
- Digital: personal process archive
- Travel: temporary cross-domain situation mode

## 5. Glow Current penetrates every depth

Glow Current remains the one app-level navigation runtime at every depth. Deep routes may register local Navigation Identity and contextual destinations, but they may not rebuild a top world row, sidebar, bottom tab bar, breadcrumb runtime, page-specific Shakti, or competing global navigation.

The shared Glow Thread, Return Anchor, World Fold, Reverse the Current, depth signal, Shakti continuity and route transition survive every sub-subpage.

Cross-domain movement should follow Glow Graph relationships where useful. Examples:

- Tretinoin → Tonight's Skincare Routine
- Dress → Saturday Event
- Event → Outfit
- Outfit → Hair Look
- Hair Look → Products
- Product → Shopping History

## 6. Today rooms are temporal rooms, not filters

Today Home answers: **What kind of day is this? → What matters now? → What is coming? → What should I do next?** It composes schedule, energy, routines, weekly theme, appointments, goals, work, beauty, fitness, food, home, unfinished work and current situations without turning them into independent widgets.

Morning, Between/Midday, Afternoon, Evening and Night are distinct temporal rooms with different density and behavior.

- Morning: progressive opening flow; wake, energy, sleep, first commitment, food, beauty, fitness, before-leaving needs.
- Between/Midday: transition intelligence; completed, missed, reset, next commitment, available time, energy and realistic remainder.
- Evening: closure transition; obligations, routines, dinner, beauty, home reset, tomorrow preparation, social plans and reflection.
- Night: quietest state; Close Today → Care → Prepare Tomorrow → Sleep.

Recovery states reduce visible density. High-energy states may reveal more optional depth without changing structural location.

## 7. Plan and Calendar are future-state environments

Plan is the future-state manipulation environment for the digital twin, not a generic productivity dashboard.

Calendar must reason with routine density, travel time, energy requirements, work, appointments, beauty maintenance, fitness recovery, weekly themes and dependencies. Calendar events are Glow Objects and can expose preparation, people, place, routines, documents, expenses, travel, outfit, beauty preparation, follow-up and history.

## 8. Routines are living multidimensional Glow Objects

Routine Library is a multidimensional routine universe rather than a card category library.

Every Routine can be indexed simultaneously by:

- Time of Day
- Frequency
- Life Area
- Energy
- Situation

Daily Life visualizes the rhythm Morning → Between → Afternoon → Evening → Night.

Routine Overview and Routine Player are separate experiences. The Player enters a Protected Chamber and prioritizes current step, progress, previous/next context, skip, delay, modify, low-energy substitution, contextual Ask Glow and truthful completion.

Domain routines use domain-native structures:

- Skincare: AM/PM, actives, compatibility, treatment nights, goals, owned products, devices, frequency and order.
- Hair: Hair Today, morning/night, wash day, maintenance, protective styles, shelf, schedule and cycles.
- Fitness: workout week, current workout, exercise sequence, sets/reps/rest, progression, recovery, muscle focus and history.
- Makeup: Look → Occasion → Face map → Products → Placement → Steps → Alternatives → Result/History.
- Beauty + Grooming: recurrence and due-soon maintenance calendar.
- Home: spatial room-based routine navigation.
- Digital: process archive.
- Travel: temporary situation mode reorganizing connected Trip objects across domains.

## 9. Life domain requirements

Life is a personal world, not an app-icon directory.

Fitness Home answers where the body/fitness journey is now. Workout Week visualizes load/recovery rhythm. Workout Day is execution-specific. Exercise Detail is a training dossier with role, target, setup, technique, mistakes, current working sets, history, progression, alternatives and relationships.

Beauty is a personal beauty archive/studio. Skincare exposes Today, Routine, Products, Treatments, Concerns, Actives, Devices, Progress, History and Knowledge as room-specific transformations, not generic primary tabs. Product Detail must preserve exact variant/strength, ownership state, open state, storage, routine position, frequency, compatibility/conflicts, purpose, purchase/usage history, results, notes and provenance, plus where else the object matters.

Gua Sha uses the face map as the dominant interface. Guided mode is one region, one movement, one direction, one count/timer with minimal competition.

Hair emphasizes current state, current style, next wash, maintenance due, active products, history, planned styles and protective-style cycle. Wash Day is an immersive sequence: pre-wash → cleanse → treat → condition → detangle → dry → style → protect.

Makeup is look-first rather than product-first. Look Detail is an editorial breakdown of face, complexion, eyes, brows, cheeks, lips, products, technique, variations and wear notes.

Closet is an active life system. It reasons over owned garments, availability, season, fit, style identity, outfit history, events, weather, laundry state, wishlist and real shopping gaps. Style Identity is an evolving editorial dossier and preserves provenance classes including SOURCE SAYS, PERSONAL PREFERENCE, STYLE TECHNIQUE, CREATOR OPINION, AESTHETIC CLAIM and GLOW SYSTEM ADDITION. Outfit Formula is a spatial composition workspace. Smart Shopping Decisions explain duplicates, outfits unlocked, style fit, wardrobe gap, budget impact, event use and owned alternatives rather than reducing the answer to a score.

Money avoids stereotypical finance-dashboard language. Baseline prioritizes comprehension of income, fixed/variable spending, debt, savings, obligations and cash-flow pressure. Purchase Decision uses simulation to compare buying, waiting, competing goals, equivalent objects and near-term effects.

Home becomes spatial and place-oriented, connecting rooms to storage, cleaning, objects, inventory, maintenance, shopping, routines and projects.

Saint is a relationship network connecting care, walks, food, appointments, supplies, photos, memories, travel implications, routines and expenses.

## 10. Brain and Create requirements

Brain represents thought, memory, knowledge and reflection as connected archival material, not folders. Imported Knowledge preserves source, original material, extracted concepts, confidence, provenance, relationships and where Glow uses the knowledge. Import Review clearly separates SOURCE SAYS, GLOW INFERRED, PERSONAL PREFERENCE and NEEDS CONFIRMATION. Identity Studio distinguishes explicit self-description from inference and preserves change over time.

Create is the most spatially free world. Projects, images, references, drafts, collections and ideas may behave like material on a studio table or canvas while still inheriting the same shell and object truth.

## 11. Ten-question completion test for every surface

Every page, subpage and sub-subpage must answer:

1. What Glow Object am I looking at?
2. Why is Glow showing it now?
3. What other objects is it connected to?
4. What does Glow know about its current state?
5. What changed since the last visit?
6. What can I actually do here?
7. What happens elsewhere in Glow if I do it?
8. What does Glow learn from the result?
9. Does the room visually express its domain instead of wearing a generic template?
10. If the page title disappeared, would the domain still be recognizable?

A page that cannot answer these is incomplete.

## 12. Migration rule

Do not delete working routes, data stores, executors or integrations to achieve this redesign.

Migration order:

1. preserve canonical data and existing URLs
2. remove duplicate page-local global navigation
3. register each room's Navigation Identity into the root shell
4. separate shared primitives from page composition
5. replace generic deep-page renderers with domain-native compositions
6. expose canonical object identity, state, relationships, provenance and history
7. wire real actions to the universal action/intelligence layer
8. verify responsive, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior
9. verify Glow Current and Shakti continuity
10. verify the ten-question completion test

## Final invariant

**Glow OS has one architecture, one Life Model, one intelligence system, one navigation physics and one design DNA, but many genuinely different spatial experiences. Shared primitives stay. Shared truth stays. Shared intelligence stays. Shared Glow Current stays. Generic room composition does not.**
