# GLOW OS — WORLD FOLD 2.0 REFERENCE LOCK

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: Governing visual and interaction law

The supplied World Fold 2.0 / The Living Fold reference is the locked target for the app-level World Fold experience.

## Core law
World Fold is not a menu, dashboard, tab switcher, or ordinary route chooser. It is a spatial reveal of one shared Life Model. Home is the root. Today is physically closest to now. Plan, Life, Beauty, Brain, and Create are lenses into the same underlying system.

## Required geography
- Upper crown: Brain, Beauty, Create.
- Middle wings: Plan, Life.
- Center: Home.
- Foreground: Today.
- World relationships are communicated through physical overlap, depth, light, and shared platform rather than connector lines.

## Required material language
- Warm ivory/champagne glass rather than cold gray glass.
- Thick blown-glass rims, refraction, internal reflection, specular highlights, caustics, bloom, subtle spectral edges, atmospheric haze, and low-contrast physical shadows.
- Each world owns an internal environmental climate: Brain blue/periwinkle, Beauty blush, Create lilac, Plan cool blue, Life sage, Today sunrise gold, Home pearl/champagne.
- Color originates inside each world and spills into surrounding space.

## Required world behavior
- Worlds are inhabitable miniature places, not branded cards.
- Hover/focus moves the selected world forward and lets surrounding worlds recede.
- Travel should feel like entering a world, with the destination atmosphere carrying into the transition.
- Animation is slow, calm, optical, and expensive-looking. No bouncy interaction language.
- Reduced-motion preferences must remain respected.

## Required shell behavior
- When World Fold opens, local room navigation must visually disappear/recede.
- The global top band becomes a quiet exhibition header: Glow OS / World Fold / Today state / Ask Glow.
- The page behind World Fold remains alive but recedes spatially.
- World Fold continues to use the one shared Glow Current runtime rather than creating a second navigation system.

## Required exhibition index
- A bottom translucent world shelf presents Home, Today, Plan, Life, Beauty, Brain, and Create.
- Every shelf item uses the same world identity as its main spatial lens and includes a miniature environmental preview.
- Footer language is locked to: GLOW OS / SAME YOU. MORE YOU. / WORLDS ARE LENSES. YOU ARE THE CENTER.

## Permanent UX principle
The user should feel that they are turning their life in space and choosing which dimension to enter. Every future World Fold redesign, migration, responsive adaptation, or visual polish must preserve this mental model.
