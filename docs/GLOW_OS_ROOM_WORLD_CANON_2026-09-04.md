## 0. Naming and identity law

- **Glow OS** is the operating system.
- **Glow** is the one continuous intelligent presence the user talks to.
- The **Glow Aura** is the visible living-light manifestation of Glow. It is not a second assistant or competing identity.
- One Glow identity. One conversation context. One owner for each action.
- Glow remains present through Today, Plan, Life, Brain, Create, and every room within them.

## 1. Core world law

**Every Glow OS page is a different room in the same world.**

Glow OS is not a collection of webpages, app screens, dashboards, cards, or sheets. Worlds, rooms, surfaces, and objects exist inside one continuous intelligent medium made from Glow Matter.

The user should feel that they are:
- traveling through one spatial world;
- following a current through liquid crystal;
- crossing thresholds between connected climates;
- moving the camera toward information;
- watching the current material reorganize;
- preserving conversation, position, unfinished work, and context.

### Hierarchy

- **World:** major climate: Today, Plan, Life, Brain, Create.
- **Room:** specialized environment within a world.
- **Surface:** active working area.
- **Object:** information with physical behavior.
- Focusing on an object may unfold it into a surface or room.
- Pulling back reveals its connection to the larger world.

## 4. Glow Matter physics

Glow Matter is the permanent fifth-state medium of Glow OS.

### Light has weight

Important information draws illumination toward itself. Light gathers around:
- the current moment;
- active decisions;
- unfinished or relevant actions;
- the touched/spoken-about object;
- Glow intelligence;
- information requiring attention.

Lower-priority information recedes through depth, diffusion, and lower material clarity instead of adding visual clutter.

### Interaction has momentum

- Dragging time creates a controlled wake.
- Moving a task leaves a brief light trail.
- Opening a memory creates a gentle depth shift.
- Connecting notes produces visible spatial tension.
- Completing something releases stored light.
- Reorganization causes nearby objects to make room naturally.

Motion is damped, controlled, and non-bouncy.

### Information has physical behavior

## 5. Spatial continuity

Glow OS must preserve three kinds of continuity.

### Positional continuity

The system remembers where the user came from through a light trail, spatial edge, anchor, miniature previous context, nearby threshold, horizon, or reversible gesture.

### Context continuity

The object that caused a transition stays connected to its origin. Examples:
- task from Today remains tied to its time;
- recipe from Grocery retains ingredient context;
- hair routine opened from Calendar retains appointment and leave-ready context;
- memory opened from a person retains relationship context;
- note converted to project retains its source thread.

### Conversation continuity

Glow remembers:
- what the user asked;
- the selected object;
- origin room;
- recent conversation;
- open proposal;
- pending approval;
- current goal;
- unfinished work.

## 6. Movement language

Glow OS uses four primary spatial movements:

1. **Focus** — camera moves closer; object becomes working environment.
2. **Reveal** — related information becomes visible around current object.
3. **Drift** — gentle movement between nearby connected areas.
4. **Transform** — current material reorganizes into another functional state.

Objects may become places without feeling like new pages opened.

## 7. Five-world navigation law

The five-world control remains stable for orientation but does not behave like a page switcher.

It communicates:
- current world;
- nearby connected world;
- direction of movement;
- unfinished context;
- persistent Glow presence.

Tapping Today / Plan / Life / Brain / Create moves the user toward that region through material transformation.

On iPhone, movement may compress for usability but must preserve shared-object continuity. On iPad, more surrounding context should remain visible to strengthen spatial orientation.

## 8. Responsive navigation rail law

The primary rail uses three persistent states and must never allow labels to spill outside its material boundary.

### Full rail
- wide desktop / wide iPad;
- icon + small centered label;
- clear selected material state.

### Compact rail
- iPad Split View and tighter layouts;
- slightly wider physical rail than the old collapsed state;
- smaller bounded label box;
- maximum two lines where necessary;
- labels stay inside the rail;
- **Library** is the product-facing label instead of Resources in the compact Today rail.

### Ultra-compact rail
- very narrow states;
- icon-first by default;
- selected/focused label may reveal beside the rail;
- no text overflow;
- no microscopic touch targets.

This responsive law applies to future spatial rails unless a room has a deliberate alternative navigation form.

## 9. Five main worlds

### 9.1 Today — The Living Center

**Human question:** What matters now, and how should the rest of today flow?

**Climate:** pearlescent dawn, ivory, restrained blush/champagne, pale sky blue, warm natural light, sheer-curtain reflections.

**Spatial metaphor:** living day / temporal river.

**Structure:** NOW, NEXT, LATER, TONIGHT, TOMORROW preview. NOW is closest and brightest. Future information becomes softer and deeper.

**Core intelligence:** What Now, capacity, energy, Top 3, appointments, routines due, time remaining, leave-ready countdown, one-tap replan.

**Motion:** the day drifts like sunlight crossing a room. Completed moments dissolve into warm light behind the user.

**Glow behavior:** brightest and most attentive near NOW.

### 9.2 Plan — The Time Observatory

**Human question:** How should time be arranged before I commit?

**Climate:** lilac-blue daylight, frosted pearl, cool silver, controlled water reflections, clearer edges, stronger architecture.

**Spatial metaphor:** observatory / orbital time surface.

**Structure:** Today centered; near future close; later dates farther away; deadlines as illuminated landmarks.

**Core intelligence:** Calendar, Tasks, Reminders, Planning, Goals, Projects, Routines, Habits, schedule comparison, conflict detection, future-self scenarios.

**Motion:** deliberate horizontal/orbital movement. Dragging creates a subtle water-like wake.

**Glow behavior:** tighter, more precise symmetry with fine lines pointing toward conflicts and open time.

### 9.3 Life — The Personal House

**Human question:** What does this part of my life need right now?

**Climate:** changes room by room while preserving Glow Matter physics.

**Spatial metaphor:** connected private house / suite.

**Rooms:** Body, Beauty, Closet, Food, Home, Money, Work, Relationships, Travel and related subrooms.

**Motion:** crossing thresholds. Light and sound change gradually.

**Glow behavior:** adapts by room: warmer in Body, reflective in Beauty, clearer in Money, practical in Work.

### 9.4 Brain — The Inner Universe

**Human question:** What connects, matters, or is emerging in my inner world?

**Climate:** midnight indigo, smoky violet, pearl-white light, faint silver connections, cinematic twilight, deeper shadow.

**Spatial metaphor:** inner universe / knowledge constellation.

**Information forms:** constellations, timelines, threads, clusters, film strips, thought clouds, connected webs.

**Core intelligence:** Notes Web, Memories, Thoughts, Ideas, Insights, Observations, Connections, Personal Timeline, Decision Archive, Brain Graph, Pattern Cinema.

**Motion:** zoom, drift, focus, connection. Never filing-cabinet scrolling.

**Glow behavior:** lantern-like. Relevant connections brighten; unrelated information remains softly dark.

### 9.5 Create — The Transformation Studio

**Human question:** What am I making, and what should this become?

**Climate:** bright neutral pearl, studio daylight, pale champagne tools, restrained prismatic color, open working space.

**Spatial metaphor:** atelier / transformation workspace.

**Core intelligence:** Capture, Creative Studio, Writing, image creation, visual cards, mood boards, Notes, Inbox, Import, Gmail, file transformation, Conversation-to-World Builder.

**Motion:** Mist → Structure. Spoken or captured ideas condense into notes, pages, images, plans, projects, or other objects.

**Glow behavior:** opens wider; prismatic edges become more visible while creating.

## 10. Today room canon

### Morning Brief
Quiet ceremonial opening. Layered curtain reveal. Customizable wake time, voice/silent mode, brief length, medication/supplement prompts, weather, outfit, breakfast, routine, first appointment, encouragement style. Must never feel like a report.

### Today Main
Live control surface. Customizable productivity mode, information density, visible life areas, Top 3 format, time-window boundaries, calendar visibility, energy display, Glow initiative. Only the present moment is fully illuminated.

### Evening Debrief
Gentle closing room, never a performance scorecard. Supports spoken/written reflection, photo memory, self-love journal, spending review, tomorrow preview, carry-forward/release. Climate shifts to candlelit pearl + twilight blue; completed moments become soft light points.

## 11. Plan room canon

### Calendar
Architectural map of time. Supports column/ribbon/day/week/month/spatial views. Events are translucent time vessels. Travel and preparation appear as connected shadows. Free time is open breathing space. Intelligence: rearrange day, protect time, find better time, compare schedules, reveal hidden preparation.

### Tasks
Action workshop organized by readiness, not only list order: Ready now, Waiting, Needs preparation, Quick relief, Deep focus, Can wait, Blocked. Completion compresses into a light pearl that joins the accomplishment trail.

### Reminders
Quiet safety net. Reminders attach to time/place/person/routine instead of imitating tasks. Glow avoids nagging when timing is clearly wrong.

### Planning Studio
Large movable possibility surface for daily/weekly/monthly planning, scenario comparison, guided questions, auto draft, manual planning. Nothing becomes real until approved.

### Goals
Horizon, not checklist. Goals are illuminated landmarks; projects/habits form paths. Landmark becomes clearer and closer as real evidence accumulates.

### Projects
Central working table surrounded by next actions, notes, files, timeline, decisions, people, inspiration, blockers.

### Routines
Guided pathway with one active step illuminated. Supports full/quick/low-energy versions, spoken guidance, timers, music, products, pause/skip behavior. Skipping a step intelligently changes what follows.

### Habits
Rhythm garden, not streak scoreboard. Habits grow through light, rings, and atmosphere. Missing a day never produces a damaged/dying visual.

## 12. Life room canon

### Body
Calm body-awareness room. Warm blush-peach pearl, diffuse light, slower motion. Central body state with sleep, energy, symptoms, medication, movement, care. Body-neutral language and privacy controls are mandatory.

### Fitness
Active movement studio. Warm morning light, pale coral, clean silver, stronger rhythm. Workout days are large doors; opening one reveals ordered steps. Motion feels grounded and rhythmic.

### Wellness
Restoration room. Soft peach, mineral white, water, breathing light. Rest, sleep, nervous-system support, hydration, mood, check-ins, appointments, recovery. Language sensitivity and professional-care reminders supported.

### Beauty Command Center
Getting-ready intelligence room. Vanity daylight, polished ivory, mirror reflections, champagne metal. Organizes Skin, Hair, Makeup, Body, Fragrance, Final Look around today’s event and leave-ready time.

### Makeup Studio
Precision face atelier. Face and selected look central; steps surround it in application order. Supports product ownership, skin tone, finish, coverage, occasion, time, placement maps, camera mirror mode, lighting simulation. Glow may create/adapt visual face maps.

### Skincare
Calm treatment laboratory. Milk glass, cool water, soft clinical clarity without medical coldness. AM/PM routines orbit current skin state. Product/prescription compatibility and safety outrank aesthetics.

### Hair Studio
Private salon + archive. Warm ivory, mirror light, glossy reflections. Today’s hair state, style plan, wash schedule, routine pathway, products, history, inspiration. Routine movement follows the physical order hair is handled.

### Gua Sha Studio
Guided facial movement room. Water, jade-gray, warm skin light, quiet reflection. Large face guide with directional paths. Mirror Mode reverses directions for easy follow-along.

### Closet
Translucent personal wardrobe. Clear glass, ivory fabric, silver rails, daylight. Clothing hangs/layers spatially rather than repeated database cards. Glow coordinates weather, occasion, hair, makeup, and prep time.

### Food
Warm personal kitchen. Cream stone, pale wood, afternoon light, subtle herb color. Organized around Eat now, Cook, Prepare, Use soon, Shop, Saved recipes.

### Recipes
Live cooking surface. Only current step remains large; required ingredients appear beside it. Supports serving size, units, voice, hands-free, timers, substitutions, screen awake. Finished recipe becomes a plated memory with personal changes preserved.

### Grocery
Fast practical shopping companion. Brighter than Recipes. Reorganizes by store, aisle, recipe, urgency, budget, household area.

### Home
Living floor plan. Polished stone, warm daylight, architectural calm. Physical rooms are navigation. Each room contains cleaning, maintenance, inventory, design projects.

### Money
Private clarity chamber. Cool mint, ivory ledger, clean silver, almost no decorative motion. Available, Committed, Upcoming, Saving, Flexible spending, Financial attention. Glow becomes smaller/more precise so numbers remain primary.

### Work
Composed professional studio. Neutral pearl, navy-gray shadow, restrained champagne. Changes according to employment, applications, interviews, career building. Supports schedule, roles, managers, contacts, dress rules, pay, goals, availability, privacy.

### Relationships / People
Warm human connection space. Rose-beige, candlelit pearl, photographic depth. People appear through relationship, memories, conversations, promises, dates. Never use engagement scores as relationship quality.

### Travel
Moving horizon. Sky light, pearl luggage surfaces, watery map reflections. Journey stages: Dreaming, Deciding, Booking, Preparing, Traveling, Remembering.

## 13. Brain room canon

### Notes
Refined writing desk. Note stays central; connections remain marginal until requested. Supports typography, width, background, folders, tags, backlinks, focus, handwriting, AI initiative.

### Notes Web
Navigable knowledge constellation. Connected luminous forms. Size means depth/importance, not popularity.

### Memory
Cinematic personal archive. Twilight film, subtle grain, muted color, depth. Memories appear as photos, film frames, sounds, places, atmospheres. Sensitive-memory controls required.

### Thoughts
Unstructured inner holding space. Thoughts may remain floating fragments without forced filing. Supports delayed organization, private mode, emotional labels, voice capture, whether Glow responds, whether thought may become task.

### Ideas
Incubation chamber. States: Spark → Seed → Structure → Prototype → Active creation → Completed concept.

### Insights
Quiet observatory. Glow presents patterns only when supported by evidence. Supports minimum evidence, time range, sensitivity, language style, whether suggestions are included.

### Timeline
Navigable river of personal time. Past flows behind, present glows center, plans extend lightly ahead.

### Decision Archive
Resolved crossroads chamber. Branching paths with chosen route illuminated. Preserves alternatives, reasons, expected result, review date, actual result, lessons, privacy.

### Brain Graph
Complete map of inner world. Notes, people, projects, memories, goals, media use different forms, not merely color changes.

## 14. Create room canon

### Capture
Fastest entrance. One open field accepts speech, text, photo, video, file, scan, link, handwriting. Supports privacy, auto placement, review-before-save, transcription, shortcuts.

### Creative Studio
Open transformation workspace. Current creation dominates screen. Tools appear only when relevant. Supports image, writing, mood board, layout, plan, cards, presentation, style refs, output size.

### Inbox
Temporary arrival room. Items visibly move toward suggested destinations. Supports confidence threshold, review frequency, bulk action, source, expiration, archive behavior.

### Import
Careful migration room. Shows source → extracted information → suggested destination → duplicates → conflicts → approval. Preserves detail, privacy, duplicate rules, original file behavior.

## 15. Utility room canon

### Search
Casting light across the entire world. Supports fuzzy spelling, natural language, image recognition, voice, time memory, suggested correction, world-scoped search, person/mood/location. Results group by meaning, not only file type.

### Attention Center
Composed briefing room. Separates: Needs attention now, Soon, Waiting for someone, Possible concern, Quiet suggestion, Safe to ignore. User controls Glow assertiveness.

### Concierge
Private planning salon for cross-domain situations: trips, appointments, interviews, events, shopping, major projects, life changes. Uses one evolving master plan, not disconnected cards.

### Settings
Intentionally quiet and neutral: neutral pearl, no atmospheric distraction, minimal motion, maximum clarity. Categories include appearance, climates, motion, voice, Aura intensity, Glow personality, AI initiative, privacy, data, notifications, accessibility, integrations, navigation, page visibility, experimental features.

## 18. Glow signature intelligence moment

Whenever Glow materially understands or changes something, an **aurora ripple** travels through Glow Matter.

For actions:
1. ripple begins at Glow / Glow Aura;
2. travels through the continuous surface;
3. reaches the affected object;
4. reorganizes the material/information;
5. resolves into a visible receipt.

The ripple is soft, controlled, functional, recognizable, and never decorative sparkle/loading chrome.

## 19. Acceptance criteria for every future room

A new or redesigned Glow room is not complete unless:

- it clearly belongs to the same Glow world;
- it has a unique room identity and spatial metaphor;
- Glow is present and context-continuous;
- its climate is distinct yet uses the same physics;
- major navigation does not feel like browser page replacement;
- its objects have behavior that matches their meaning;
- iPhone and iPad layouts intentionally recompose;
- nothing important is clipped or inaccessible;
- text remains crisp and readable;
- reduced motion preserves hierarchy and use;
- completion behavior is meaningful to the room;
- its primary human question is obvious;
- it does not default to generic cards, dashboard grids, or repeated layout templates;
- unsupported meaningful mutations still require Glow’s approval workflow.
