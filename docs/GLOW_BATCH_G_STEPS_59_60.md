# Glow OS — Batch G, Steps 59–60

## Step 59 — Whole-Glow Convergence Sweep
Glow now has a convergence model that groups repository-wide violations by the highest shared owner. Duplicate navigation routes to Global Shell, duplicate identities to the canonical-object layer, local architecture to the template factory, duplicate state/overlay systems to their factories, CSS/token bypasses to environment tokens, context bypasses to Glow Context, disconnected history to the history layer, and route/data/UI gaps to their proper owners.

The governing repair strategy is ancestor-first: one shared-root fix should repair every inheriting surface instead of patching descendants individually. Legacy retirement remains gated by parity and QA.

## Step 60 — Factory Lock + Glow Generator
A generator contract now turns a requested experience into an identity, inheritance chain, master template, canonical-object dependencies, shared capabilities, exception status and required QA. New experiences should therefore be generated from the Glow system rather than starting with custom page architecture.

Factory Lock is evidence-based. It cannot report locked until all family waves are accounted for, convergence has no blockers, Global Shell/canonical objects/templates/shared engines/state/overlay/data/history are verified, runtime is clean, responsive/accessibility pass and Golden exceptions are approved.

Batch G adds the convergence and generator/lock machinery. It deliberately does not claim the whole live application has already passed those gates, does not delete legacy surfaces, and does not merge or ship main.
