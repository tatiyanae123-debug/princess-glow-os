# Glow OS — Continuous World Master Experience Layer

**Status:** Current master page-experience specification, September 2, 2026.  
**Authority:** This layer is subordinate to and compatible with the existing Glow OS constitution. It strengthens how rooms express the system. It does not erase data, workflows, provenance, safety, approval, accessibility, or the five-world foundation.

## Constitutional foundation

Glow OS is one continuous living world. Technical routes are addresses and state changes, not visual page replacements.

The five primary worlds are permanently:

1. Today
2. Plan
3. Life
4. Brain
5. Create

No Life subsystem, utility, database, or temporary workflow may become a sixth primary world.

## Shared Glow language

All rooms remain visibly part of one system through:

- Living Glow Aura as embedded intelligence, never a chatbot orb, mascot, fairy, person, or literal angel.
- Glow Matter as responsive liquid-crystal / pearl / polished-stone material behavior.
- Ivory pearl, polished stone, champagne metal, restrained water reflection, natural refraction, refined typography, and Apple-level restraint.
- Permanent Ask Glow, consistent gestures, save/undo/search/action receipts, contextual voice, and target-travel feedback.
- iPhone and iPad-first usability with desktop support.
- Reduced-motion support and dyslexia-friendly presentation controls.
- Non-shaming health, body, money, productivity, and relationship behavior.
- No false actions, silent destructive changes, or unapproved consequential rearrangements.

## The room law

Every important Glow room must be differentiated across these dimensions:

**climate → space → physics → intelligence → information → controls → completion → sound → Aura behavior**

A room is incomplete if it is merely a recolored template.

## Anti-repetition QA

Every finished screen must pass all of these questions:

- Different dominant spatial metaphor?
- Signature interaction?
- Unique light climate?
- Meaningful motion model?
- Different completion behavior?
- Distinct Glow role?
- Different information exploration?

- Avoids copied card structures?

## World definitions

### Today — Living Center

Today represents the present. Its spatial law is temporal depth:

**Now → Next → Later → Tonight → Tomorrow**

Now has maximum clarity. Future layers become smaller, softer, lower contrast, farther away, and less demanding. Completed time leaves warm residual light rather than becoming another checkbox grid.

Today must never regress into a conventional widget dashboard.

### Plan — Time Observatory

Plan is spatial time, not a bundle of calendar/task/project apps. Today is physically closest; tomorrow is nearby; distant time recedes. Deadlines become landmarks, projects occupy temporal territory, and Glow can project conflicts, preparation shadows, protected time, alternatives, and future-self scenarios.

Planning remains non-destructive. Glow may simulate and propose. Nothing becomes real until approved.

### Life — Personal House

Life contains lived domains, each with its own habitat and physical behavior. Beauty, Body, Fitness, Wellness, Food, Closet, Home, Money, Work, Relationships, Travel, and other domains are rooms in Life, never replacements for the five-world structure.

Key room identities include:

- Body: body-neutral silhouette-centered state, never body grading.
- Fitness: rhythmic guided movement with one active exercise dominating.
- Wellness: restoration room with low information velocity and clear separation between support, professional-care reminders, source claims, and actual medical instructions.
- Beauty Command Center: event-oriented orchestration across Skin → Hair → Makeup → Body → Fragrance → Final Look.
- Makeup Studio: face as workspace, placement maps, lighting/mirror behavior, owned products kept distinct from recommendations.
- Skincare: state-centered, mode-aware routines with compatibility and provider instructions overriding aesthetics.
- Hair Studio: directional handling, sectioning, maintenance timelines, spatial product shelf.
- Gua Sha Studio: guided Mirror Mode with flipped direction, labels, animation, and voice.
- Closet: wardrobe spatial logic, not commerce/admin grids.
- Food: action habitats such as Eat Now, Cook, Prepare, Use Soon, Shop, Saved Recipes.
- Recipes: current step dominates, ingredient relevance is local, timers attach to originating steps, voice/hands-free first.
- Grocery: fast, bright, high-contrast navigation and aisle/store optimization.
- Home: actual room/floor-plan navigation connected to exact product-storage homes.
- Money: quiet cool-mint + ivory ledger climate, restrained motion, numbers first, no finance spectacle.
- Work: reconfigures around current employment, applications, interviews, scheduling, career planning, sales, and professional contacts.
- Relationships: memories, dates, promises, gifts, boundaries, and follow-ups without relationship scoring.
- Travel: Dreaming → Deciding → Booking → Preparing → Traveling → Remembering.

### Brain — Depth World

Brain must never become Notion. Information types have different habitats:

- Notes = desk
- Notes Web = constellation
- Memory = cinema
- Thoughts = floating holding field
- Ideas = incubation
- Insights = observatory
- Timeline = river
- Decision Archive = crossroads
- Brain Graph = universe-scale relational exploration

Thoughts may remain unfinished. Insights require evidence and may explicitly say there is not enough evidence. Historical events and speculative future plans remain visually distinct. Sensitive memories never resurface merely for engagement.

### Create — Mist to Structure

Create is not a collection of AI tools. Its governing transformation is:

**vague → legible → structured → tangible**

Capture accepts input before classification. Creative Studio lets the active medium dominate. Inbox is temporary and visibly routes objects toward destinations. Import exposes source, extraction, proposed meaning, duplicate/conflict state, destination, and approval.

## Universal utilities

- Search behaves like illumination across situations rather than file-type buckets.
- Attention Center uses: Needs attention now / Soon / Waiting for someone / Possible concern / Quiet suggestion / Safe to ignore.
- Concierge orchestrates complex multi-domain situations as one evolving situation.
- Settings is intentionally the least atmospheric room: neutral pearl, minimal movement, maximum legibility.

## Page-level personalization

Rooms may override density, atmosphere, motion, Aura intensity, spoken guidance, text size, dyslexia support, contrast, color intensity, spatial/card mode, AI initiative, visible sections, default perspective, privacy, and shortcuts.

Personalization changes presentation and assistance without destroying room identity. Brain cannot be customized until it becomes Money; Today cannot be customized into a spreadsheet.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not regress Glow into:

- ordinary dashboards
- identical rounded-card collections
- conventional push/page-flip/slide navigation
- hard climate cuts
- generic AI/chatbot UI
- Siri-like orb
- mascot/fairy/angel character
- neon/fairycore/Y2K/glitter/crystal fantasy
- glassmorphism everywhere
- repeated hero layouts
- one sidebar plus interchangeable database tables as the product model
- gratuitous animation
- points/streak punishment
- shame-driven health/body/money indicators
- silent destructive changes
- false certainty or false action receipts

## Deepest rule

**Intelligence should feel like a natural phenomenon rather than a chatbot. Information should feel like it has a natural habitat rather than a database location.**

A Glow screen is not finished merely because it is beautiful or functional. It must preserve the constitution, maintain continuous-world behavior, retain existing information and safety rules, possess its own room identity, and still unmistakably belong to Glow.
