# Glow OS — Living Liquid-Crystal Constitution

This document is permanent design law for Glow OS. Every current and future screen should feel like part of one calm intelligent medium, never like a collection of unrelated apps.

## North star

Glow is a living digital universe made from one signature material and one set of interaction physics. Each page has its own climate, but typography, spacing, motion, depth, behavior and intelligence remain unmistakably Glow.

The interface stays white-dominant, highly legible and operationally calm. The difference is that the white space is alive with restrained refraction, pearlescence, depth and water-like light.

## Glow Matter

Glow Matter is a refined liquid-crystal material suspended in clear water. It is:
- translucent, not cloudy
- pearlescent, not metallic
- refractive, not glossy
- dimensional, not shadow-heavy
- responsive, not decorative
- calm at rest

Cards, sheets, controls and fields can inherit Glow Matter while preserving contrast and task clarity.

## Shared physics

Every room obeys the same physical language:
- touch creates a subtle liquid-crystal ripple on interactive surfaces
- ambient caustic light drifts beneath the interface
- meaningful Glow intelligence produces a soft aurora sweep
- information can condense from mist into structure
- completed states may dissolve or soften into light
- motion uses gentle momentum rather than bounce
- intelligence can appear through light, reordering, connection and depth instead of another widget
- all motion respects reduced-motion preferences

## Page climates

### Today
Pearlescent dawn: rose, lilac and faint champagne. Awake, lucid and optimistic.

### Plan
Calendar, Tasks, Planning, Reminders, Routines, Habits, Goals and Projects use cool lilac-blue temporal light. Architectural, precise and forward-moving.

### Body and care
Fitness, Wellness, Food and recovery experiences use mineral water, sage and clear blue light. Fresh, physical and restorative.

### Beauty
Beauty, Makeup, Skincare, Hair, Beauty Lab and Closet use rose pearl, champagne and violet opal. Warm, tactile and elegant.

### Money
Finance and Financial Brain use emerald mineral light with restrained gold. Grounded and trustworthy.

### Intelligence
Brain, Concierge, Memory, Timeline, Observations, Graph, Connections and Notices use violet-blue neural/celestial light. Deep and connected.

### Create
Capture, Creative Studio, Notes, Inbox, Gmail, Import and related creation tools use shifting opalescent light, like an idea forming inside crystal.

### Home and World
Botanical mineral light, earth pearl and restrained lavender. Spatial, atmospheric and lived-in.

### Work and system
Work uses clear slate crystal. Settings and system surfaces use restrained opal. Focused and controlled.

## Intelligence as a natural phenomenon

Glow intelligence should not require a chatbot-shaped object everywhere. It may be expressed through:
- aurora sweeps
- refraction changes
- connected constellations
- light trails between related information
- spatial reordering
- mist-to-structure transitions
- a relevant object surfacing to the foreground

The universal Ask Glow action layer remains the explicit place for conversation and commands.

## Depth architecture

Use three meaningful optical depths:
1. Atmosphere: climate, caustics and ambient world physics.
2. Living surface: current information, cards, fields and controls.
3. Attention: active intelligence, focus actions, urgent states and modal surfaces.

Depth must communicate relevance. Do not add floating layers for decoration alone.

## Seasonal evolution

Glow may subtly change atmospheric temperature with season, time of day or context. Structure, semantic colors, navigation and contrast remain stable. Seasonality changes atmosphere, not usability.

## Intelligence event contract

The global world-physics layer recognizes these document events:
- `glow:intelligence`
- `glow:command-complete`
- `glow:action-complete`

Dispatch one after a meaningful intelligent completion to trigger the universal aurora response. Do not use the signature aurora for trivial clicks.

## Existing usability law remains in force

This constitution extends the Glow OS white-editorial rulebook. It does not replace its usability rules. Preserve:
- white-dominant operational rooms
- low visible density
- progressive disclosure
- Now / Next / Later
- one universal Glow assistant/action entry
- simplified navigation
- 8px spacing grid
- Focus Mode
- real functional controls only
- iPhone, iPad and desktop responsiveness
- keyboard/focus accessibility
- reduced-motion support

## Acceptance filter

Before a Glow screen ships, it should pass all of these tests:
1. Can the user immediately tell where they are?
2. Is what matters now obvious?
3. Is the next useful action clear?
4. Does the screen feel made from Glow Matter rather than generic app UI?
5. Does its climate belong to its domain without becoming a separate theme?
6. Does it obey the same physics as the rest of Glow?
7. Does intelligence reduce complexity rather than add noise?
8. Is it accessible and calm?
9. Could this screen plausibly belong only to Glow?

If the last answer is no, the design is not finished.
