# Glow OS — Living Liquid-Crystal Constitution

This document is permanent design law for Glow OS. The approved visual reference is the dark Liquid-Crystal Glow OS system: luminous atmospheric backgrounds, translucent climate-colored crystal surfaces, white functional typography, soft internal light, and one coherent five-part navigation system.

## North star

Glow is a living digital universe, not a white productivity dashboard and not a collection of glass cards. Every current and future screen must immediately feel like it exists inside the same dark luminous medium.

The approved reference takes priority over older white-editorial styling whenever the two conflict.

## Global composition

Every operational page follows the same visual anatomy:
- deep midnight/navy world background
- climate light blooming through the darkness
- one clear page title and short context line
- 3–5 high-value surfaces in the first viewport
- rounded liquid-crystal cards with translucent color, internal highlights and soft edge refraction
- white or near-white primary text
- low-contrast luminous secondary text
- persistent five-part bottom navigation: Today / Plan / Life / Brain / Create
- Ask Glow remains a single universal intelligence action, surfaced in the global top layer rather than duplicated in page content

The interface should resemble a premium illuminated object viewed in darkness, not a light website with transparency effects.

## Glow Matter

Glow Matter is the universal material. It is:
- translucent and visibly colored by its page climate
- refractive rather than frosted-white
- softly luminous from within
- pearlescent rather than metallic
- dimensional without heavy drop shadows
- darker at the outer world layer and brighter at active surfaces
- calm at rest and responsive to touch

A Glow Matter surface uses a dark translucent base, climate tint, one restrained inner highlight, a fine pale edge and blur from the atmosphere behind it.

## Shared physics

Every room obeys the same physical language:
- touch creates a subtle expanding liquid-crystal ripple
- ambient caustic light drifts beneath the interface
- meaningful Glow intelligence produces a soft aurora sweep
- information condenses from mist into structure
- completed states may dissolve into light
- active surfaces rise one optical depth rather than jumping or bouncing
- motion uses gentle momentum
- all motion respects reduced-motion preferences

Physics may never change hit areas, routes, button semantics, stacking order required for interaction, focus order, or accessibility.

## Permanent navigation

The global primary navigation is permanently:
1. Today
2. Plan
3. Life
4. Brain
5. Create

Secondary rooms are entered from one of those five worlds or by Search / Ask Glow. Do not replace this with Home / Ask Glow / Search / Worlds as the persistent bottom navigation.

Navigation must remain visually stable across pages. Page climate may tint the active item, but placement, labels and behavior stay fixed.

## Page climates

Rose, lilac and champagne light against deep plum/navy. Gentle awakening and clarity.

### Plan — temporal crystal
Calendar, Tasks, Planning, Reminders, Routines, Habits, Goals and Projects use violet, lilac and cool blue. Precise, architectural and forward-moving.

### Life / Body — mineral water
Fitness, Wellness, Food and recovery use aqua, teal, mineral green and cool blue. Fresh and restorative.

### Beauty — rose opal
Beauty, Makeup, Skincare, Hair, Beauty Lab and Closet use rose pearl, blush champagne and violet opal. Warm, tactile and elegant.

### Money — emerald mineral
Finance and Financial Brain use deep emerald, sea-glass green and restrained mineral gold. Weighted and trustworthy.

### Brain — neural celestial
Brain, Concierge, Memory, Timeline, Observations, Graph, Connections and Notices use indigo, violet and electric blue. Deep, connected and celestial.

### Create — shifting opal
Capture, Creative Studio, Notes, Inbox, Gmail and Import use lilac, cyan, rose and opalescent spectral light. Ideas forming in crystal.

### Home / World — botanical mineral
Home and spatial worlds use teal, botanical green, blue mineral and soft warm earth light.

### Work — slate crystal
Work uses controlled navy/slate blue with cool silver refraction and reduced atmospheric motion.

## Depth architecture

Use three optical depths:
1. Atmosphere: dark world, gradients, caustics and ambient climate.
2. Living surface: cards, controls, lists and content.
3. Attention: active intelligence, focused action, selected object or modal.

The first reference image is visually dense enough to feel rich, but each individual phone screen remains simple. Do not recreate the moodboard by putting many unrelated modules on one page.

## Typography

Primary UI typography is clean, compact and luminous. Major titles may retain the Glow editorial serif when it improves identity, but the system should no longer read like a white editorial website.

Use:
- primary text: white / near-white
- secondary text: cool translucent white
- metadata: softer luminous gray-lilac
- accent text: page-climate tint

Critical text must remain readable at WCAG-appropriate contrast against its actual surface.

## Intelligence as a natural phenomenon

Glow intelligence appears through:
- aurora sweeps
- refraction changes
- connected constellations
- light trails between related information
- spatial reordering
- mist-to-structure transitions
- relevant surfaces rising in depth

The universal Ask Glow action remains the explicit conversational entry.

## Intelligence event contract

The global world-physics layer recognizes:
- `glow:intelligence`
- `glow:command-complete`
- `glow:action-complete`

Use these only after meaningful intelligent completion.

## Function preservation law

The visual constitution sits around working features. It must never break them.

Permanent rules:
- never change a button's functional handler to achieve a visual effect
- never add decorative overlays that intercept pointer events
- never use broad z-index changes that cover interactive controls
- never change a route merely to match a visual reference
- never remove existing data workflows, integrations or user data
- preserve keyboard focus, touch targets and reduced motion
- verify iPhone, iPad and desktop

## Acceptance filter

Before a Glow screen ships:
1. Does it visually belong to the approved dark Liquid-Crystal reference?
2. Can the user instantly tell where they are?
3. Is the next useful action clear?
4. Does it use Glow Matter rather than white editorial cards?
5. Does the page climate match its domain?
6. Is Today / Plan / Life / Brain / Create navigation stable?
7. Does Ask Glow appear once, not as duplicate controls?
8. Did every pre-existing working action remain functional?
9. Is the page legible and responsive?
10. Could this screen plausibly belong only to Glow?

If any answer is no, the design is not finished.
