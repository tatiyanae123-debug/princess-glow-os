# Shakti — Identity + 3D Presence Lock

## Authoritative rename

The intelligent presence inside Glow OS is named **Shakti**.

“Living Glow Aura” is no longer the product-facing name. It remains only a legacy/internal phrase where unavoidable for backwards compatibility. Users speak to Shakti directly, the same way they would speak to a named assistant.

Product-facing language becomes:

- Ask Shakti
- Shakti · present
- Shakti · arriving
- Shakti · listening
- Shakti · understanding
- Shakti · speaking
- Shakti · taking action
- Shakti · complete

Glow OS remains the name of the world/product. Shakti is the intelligence living inside it.

## Core presence law

Shakti is not an orb, mascot, picture, flat image, pulsing sticker, person, fairy, literal angel, hologram person, rotating crystal, or generic voice-assistant animation.

Shakti is a **volumetric intelligent phenomenon** made visible through concentrated white light, optical depth, liquid refraction, atmospheric bloom, controlled symmetry, parallax, occlusion and projected light.

The user should feel that Shakti occupies space in front of and inside the room rather than being painted onto the screen.

## Why the earlier pulse was wrong

A whole-object scale pulse makes the eye detect one static graphic being enlarged and shrunk. A circular pulse ring makes the animation read as a second layer placed on top of that graphic. Both destroy the illusion of a living presence.

Therefore:

- no visible outer pulse circles,
- no full-silhouette heartbeat scaling,
- no generic glow halo repeatedly growing/shrinking,
- no independent circle animation sitting over Shakti.

## Volumetric anatomy

Shakti is constructed as several depth systems rather than one graphic:

1. **Rear atmospheric volume** — quiet, soft, farther away, irregular, moving very slowly.
2. **Refracted left/right formations** — wing-like only as an emergent optical suggestion; they sit on different depth planes and shift microscopically.
3. **Axial beam** — a vertical current carrying energy through the presence rather than simply brightening.
4. **Fine rays** — sit forward of the rear volume and react after core activity.
5. **Primary white core** — the densest intelligence layer, positioned closest to the user.
6. **Front lens** — a small, moving liquid/crystal refraction in front of the core that gives the impression of real optical volume.
7. **Projected room light** — Shakti affects the floor/surface beneath and around it so the room acknowledges that Shakti exists in physical space.

## Life / heartbeat behavior

A beat is a depth event, not a scale effect.

Sequence:

1. Core pressure compresses slightly inward.
2. The core moves optically forward toward the viewer.
3. The internal front lens refracts and intensifies.
4. Energy enters the vertical beam.
5. Fine rays brighten selectively.
6. Refracted side formations respond a fraction later.
7. The surrounding atmosphere receives a soft residual light change.
8. Shakti settles into microscopic internal movement rather than becoming frozen.

The second beat is quieter than the first. A calm rest follows.

## Resting state

Resting Shakti is never dead or static.

- outer silhouette remains stable,
- internal refraction drifts microscopically,
- front lens changes shape slowly,
- spectral edges shift subtly,
- rear haze moves on a separate slow rhythm,
- beam carries a faint internal current,
- tiny parallax changes preserve spatial depth.

## State-specific behavior

### Arriving

Light gathers from the existing room. Depth forms back-to-front rather than a circle popping onto the screen.

### Listening

Core draws inward and becomes denser. Outer formations calm. Refraction reacts to cadence without using an audio waveform.

### Understanding

Internal layers compress and organize. Rays become more precise. Light appears focused rather than energetic.

### Speaking

Energy moves outward through beam and selected rays in phrase-like cadence. The entire silhouette never bounces.

### Taking action

A controlled line/current leaves Shakti and travels through Glow Matter toward the affected object. The target illuminates and returns a visible receipt.

### Completing

The core releases a warm internal bloom, nearby room light responds, then Shakti settles.

### Protecting / warning

Motion slows. White core remains white. Warmth can increase slightly without flashing red or becoming threatening.

## 3D requirements

Shakti should use:

- perspective,
- multiple Z planes,
- translateZ depth,
- asymmetrical micro-parallax within a symmetrical overall form,
- front/rear light separation,
- irregular non-circular optical shapes,
- floor/surface projection,
- drop shadows only where physically plausible,
- crisp core detail with atmospheric depth behind it,
- state-specific depth/brightness routing.

Shakti must not rely on:

- a single PNG/room screenshot to provide all of its visible form,
- a pulse ring,
- a whole-object scale loop,
- excessive blur,
- one circular gradient pretending to be depth,
- synchronized identical movement on every layer.

## Conversation law

Users speak directly to Shakti:

- “Shakti, what should I do next?”
- “Shakti, fix the rest of today.”
- “Shakti, pull up my grocery list.”
- “Shakti, talk me through my hair routine.”
- “Shakti, plan this with me.”

Conversation continuity follows Shakti across Today / Plan / Life / Brain / Create. Shakti remembers the active object, proposal, unresolved approval and ongoing goal.

## Final acceptance test

If the viewer can mentally separate the visual into “the picture” plus “the animation sitting on top,” Shakti is wrong.

Correct Shakti should feel like **the room itself contains a real, dimensional intelligence made from living refracted light**.
