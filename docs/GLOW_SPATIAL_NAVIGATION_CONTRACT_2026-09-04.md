# Glow OS Spatial Navigation Contract — 2026-09-04

This contract is authoritative for current and future Glow OS rooms.

## Core law

Glow OS is one continuous intelligent world. Major navigation is movement of Glow Matter, camera, focus, depth, reveal, drift, or transformation. It is not browser-page replacement disguised with styling.

## World navigation

- The outer world shell and Glow presence remain perceptually continuous.
- Today, Plan, Life, Brain, and Create are regions of one world.
- Moving between rooms uses a refractive continuity field, not a blank fade, hard cut, generic slide, page curl, or card-deck transition.
- Page-level browser scrolling is not a navigation model.
- Local scrolling is allowed only when the information itself is intrinsically long, such as a long note, source document, archive, or large record list.
- Room transitions must preserve context and orientation.

## Today time navigation

Today is a viewport-bound spatial instrument.

- NOW is the active working layer.
- NEXT, LATER, TONIGHT, and TOMORROW form one living temporal river.
- Wheel, trackpad, swipe, touch drag, direct tap, and temporal-position controls move the river.
- Motion is damped and settles into meaningful temporal positions.
- The browser document must not vertically scroll to reach NEXT, LATER, TONIGHT, or TOMORROW.
- Time bands may move in depth and position, but no information may become unreachable.

## Responsive law

Glow must recompose. It must never crop a desktop composition to fit a narrower viewport.

For iPad and Split View:

- The shell uses the actual usable viewport.
- The navigation rail narrows before content is clipped.
- The command input retains readable width.
- Capacity, Energy, and Top 3 become a lateral optical tray when three full vessels cannot fit.
- The tray uses touch/pointer pan with scrollbars visually suppressed.
- A partial next vessel may remain visible as an affordance that more material exists.
- Tapping a vessel brings its full content forward in a focused optical lens.
- Timeline event details may pan laterally inside their physical time band, but the world itself does not page-scroll.

For phone:

- Navigation recomposes to a bottom optical rail.
- NOW, command intelligence, focus, and time remain separate spatial layers.
- The desktop room is never merely scaled down.

## Physical interaction law

If an object looks physical, it must have physical behavior or clear informational meaning.

- Pearls must represent state, action, context, or transition.
- Capacity is pressable and expands to a usable capacity lens.
- Energy is pressable and expands to a check-in/adaptation lens.
- Top 3 is pressable and expands to a full priorities lens.
- Schedule objects are pressable temporal objects.
- Long press opens a temporal object on touch devices.
- Dragging a temporal object toward another time region prepares a move request when that mutation is supported.
- Calendar and other meaningful mutations remain approval-based unless a verified direct executor exists.
- Touch targets remain usable on iPad and iPhone even when visual controls are delicate.

## Temporal objects

A schedule item contains its own title, time/range, and source/context.

- Important/current objects become clearer, closer, and more refractive.
- Distant objects become softer and deeper without becoming illegible.
- Tapping an object raises it into a focused lens.
- The focused lens may expose Join, Open context, Prepare me, details, and Move through time.
- Moving an event must never silently mutate the calendar when no verified executor exists. Glow must prepare a proposal instead.

## Command deck

The What now? surface is the primary command plane.

- It may never be clipped.
- Capacity, Energy, and Top 3 may never be cut off by the room boundary.
- No mystery overflow is allowed.
- The full Top 3 must always be reachable.
- Content that cannot fit simultaneously becomes a physical lateral tray, not a hidden overflow bug.

## Motion language

The only major navigation movements are:

1. Focus
2. Reveal
3. Drift
4. Transform

Motion is slow enough to read, fast enough to feel responsive, damped, non-bouncy, and material-aware. Reduced-motion mode preserves hierarchy and depth statically.

## Prohibited

- Browser-page scrolling as world navigation
- Hard route cuts
- Blank full-screen fades
- Generic horizontal page slides
- Book/page turns
- Card-deck navigation
- Cropped command panels
- Inaccessible overflow
- Visible tiny scrollbars as the primary interaction affordance
- Decorative controls that do nothing
- Tiny touch targets
- Fake drag behavior that claims a mutation succeeded

## Engineering acceptance criteria

A spatial Glow room is not complete unless:

- the shell remains within the real viewport;
- major world movement does not require browser scrolling;
- every visible physical control has a real action or defined state;
- command content is never clipped;
- all information remains reachable by touch, pointer, keyboard, or an explicit focused lens;
- iPad Split View has a deliberate recomposition state;
- temporal navigation works by swipe/drag/wheel/tap rather than document scroll;
- unsupported mutations route through Glow approval instead of pretending to complete;
- route changes use the shared Glow Matter continuity field;
- reduced-motion behavior remains functional.
