Glow Matter is the permanent foundational medium: responsive living liquid crystal in which light has weight, interaction has momentum, and information has distinct physical behavior.

## Continuity laws

Glow OS preserves positional continuity, context continuity, and conversation continuity.

The system remembers where the user came from through light trails, spatial edges, persistent anchors, miniature previous context, nearby horizons, or reversible gestures.

The information that caused a transition remains connected to the destination. Examples include tasks tied to their original time, recipes tied to grocery ingredients, hair routines tied to appointments and leave-ready time, memories tied to people, and notes tied to project origins.

Glow follows everywhere and remembers what the user asked, what object was being discussed, what it proposed, what still needs approval, the previous world/room, and the user’s current objective.

## Four navigation movements

1. Focus: move closer to one object until it expands into a working environment.
2. Reveal: related information becomes visible around the current object.
3. Drift: move gently between nearby connected areas without leaving the world.
4. Transform: the current material reorganizes into another functional state.

Objects can become surfaces or rooms. Pulling back reveals how they connect to the larger world.
