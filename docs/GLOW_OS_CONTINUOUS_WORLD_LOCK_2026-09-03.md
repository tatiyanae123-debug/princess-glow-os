# Glow OS Continuous World Lock — September 3, 2026

Status: AUTHORITATIVE. This supersedes any prior visual or navigation rule that treats Glow OS as a conventional collection of separate screens, dashboards, webpages, page flips, or book-like surfaces.

## 1. Permanent foundation

Glow OS is one continuous intelligent living world made from Glow Matter.

Today, Plan, Life, Brain, and Create remain the five primary worlds, but they are climates/regions inside one world rather than ordinary page containers.

Navigation means transformation, movement, focus, reveal, drift, or reorganization of shared material. It does not mean replacement.

Never use page curls, card-deck transitions, hard cuts, full-screen blank fades, generic horizontal slides, repeated push-navigation, new screens flying over old screens, or a disconnected landing page for every subpage.

## 2. Glow Matter laws

Glow Matter is responsive living liquid crystal capable of holding information.

### Light has weight
Important information draws light toward itself. Current moments, relevant decisions, unfinished actions, Glow intelligence, and touched/spoken objects become more illuminated while low-priority information softly recedes.

### Interaction has momentum
Touches, drags, voice commands, completions, and reorganizations create controlled physical consequences: wakes, light trails, depth shifts, released light, tension between connected notes, and natural reflow.

### Information has distinct physical behavior
- Time flows, stretches, gathers, and recedes.
- Tasks wait, prepare, unlock, move, and settle.
- Memories surface through depth, atmosphere, and film fragments.
- Notes expand, connect, fold, and form constellations.
- Ideas condense from mist into stable structure.
- Habits repeat through rhythm, rings, and accumulated atmosphere.
- Goals exist as distant landmarks that become clearer.
- Money settles into precise stable layers.
- Routines unfold as guided pathways.
- Relationships gather around shared moments and human context.
- Intelligence appears as aurora, refraction, and reorganization.

## 4. Continuity requirements

### Positional continuity
Always preserve where the user came from using a light trail, spatial edge, persistent anchor, miniature previous context, nearby doorway/horizon, or reversible gesture.

### Context continuity
The object that caused a transition remains connected to its origin. Tasks keep their time relationship. Recipes keep ingredient connections. Hair routines retain appointments and leave-ready timing. Memories retain people context. Notes converted to projects retain the original thread.

### Conversation continuity
Ask Glow follows everywhere without restarting. Glow remembers the question, discussed object, proposal, pending approval, origin region, and active goal.

## 5. Movement vocabulary

Glow OS primarily navigates through four movements:

1. Focus — move closer to an object until it expands into a working environment.
2. Reveal — expose related information around the current object.
3. Drift — move gently between nearby connected areas without leaving the world.
4. Transform — reorganize the current material into another functional state.

An object can become a place. Pulling back reveals its connection to the larger world.

## 6. Five-world control

The persistent control remains Today / Plan / Life / Brain / Create for orientation. Tapping a world moves the user toward that region instead of replacing the current page. It should communicate current world, nearby region, direction of motion, unfinished context, and Glow's continuous presence.

On iPhone the world may compress for usability but must preserve shared-object/material continuity. On iPad more surrounding world should remain visible and spatial depth should be stronger.
